# Carbon MRV KPI Dictionary

## Terminology
- **KPI**: Key Performance Indicator
- **MRV**: Measurement, Reporting, and Verification

## Key Performance Indicators
- **Emission Reduction**: Metrics to evaluate the reduction in emissions over time.
- **Energy Consumption**: Tracking energy usage in relation to project implementation.

## Intelligence Layer
- This layer interprets data from MRV to ensure accuracy and delivery of KPIs.
