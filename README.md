# Mul-Biotech-dMRV-dashboard

**Mul Biotech Farms – Digital MRV, Carbon Intelligence & Monitoring Engine**

A comprehensive digital Measurement, Reporting, and Verification (dMRV) dashboard for Mul Biotech Farms that provides carbon intelligence, environmental monitoring, and farm performance analytics.

## 📋 Overview

This project is a monitoring and reporting system designed to track Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) for biotech farms, with particular focus on:

- **Carbon Tracking** - Monitor and report carbon sequestration and emissions
- **MRV (Measurement, Reporting, Verification)** - Digital verification of environmental claims
- **Farm Intelligence** - Real-time monitoring of farm operations and environmental conditions
- **Data Analytics** - Comprehensive dashboard for insights and reporting

## 🎯 Project Goals

- Provide transparent, verifiable carbon and environmental data
- Enable data-driven decision making for farm management
- Generate automated reports for stakeholder reporting
- Integrate satellite and ground-level monitoring data
- Support ESG and carbon credit initiatives
